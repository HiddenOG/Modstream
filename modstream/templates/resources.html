Safety resources

If you're being targeted online

What to do when you see or receive cyberbullying, hate speech or violent threats. You don't have to handle it alone.

{{ icon("alert", "icon-lg") }}
In immediate danger?

If someone threatens violence or terrorism, or you're worried about your safety or someone else's, contact your local emergency services right away. Never confront dangerous users directly.

01

Don't engage

  • Don't reply aggressively. That's often what the sender wants.
  • Step away from the screen if you need to. Your wellbeing comes first.
02

Document everything

  • Screenshot messages, including usernames, dates and times.
  • Save links to posts before they're edited or deleted.
03

Report it

  • Use the platform's built-in report tools. Most act faster on reports that include evidence.
  • Report threats and extremist content to the authorities, not just the platform.
  • If you see someone else being targeted, report it too.
04

Block and lock down

  • Block or mute repeat offenders.
  • Review privacy settings: who can message you, comment or tag you.
05

Talk to someone

  • Reach out to a friend, family member, teacher or counsellor you trust.
  • Many countries have free, confidential helplines for young people and for victims of online abuse.
