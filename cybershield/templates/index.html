How it works

Four stages, one explainable verdict

Normalize

Undo character swaps like 1d10t and $tup1d while keeping exact offsets for highlighting.

Rule layer

Word-boundary matching against tiered lists: unambiguous slurs and threats, plus context-dependent terms.

Transformer

Detoxify (BERT) scores toxicity, insults, threats, obscenity and identity attacks.

Policy

Signals combine into safe, needs review or flagged, with reasons attached.
